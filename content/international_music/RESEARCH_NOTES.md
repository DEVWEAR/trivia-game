# International Music research and editorial notes

204 original bilingual questions were researched and written on 8 October 2026. The source ledger records the precise URL observed in search or retrieved content and a factual evidence summary. Search-index evidence and retrieved page evidence are both identified by the ledger's wording; URL syntax validation alone is not factual verification or a claim that every URL was freshly fetched.

Primary sources include artist estates and official discographies, Sony and Universal release announcements, original label-supplied recording credits, the Recording Academy, the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, the Library of Congress, the Bibliothèque nationale de France and the Swedish Government. VideoStatic is a specialist trade source for the Happy video director credit; Company 3 supplies its own Dákiti production credit.

The bank includes English-language pop, soul and rock, electronic dance music, reggae, Latin and Spanish-language music, French chanson and pop, Korean pop, jazz and film songs. Recognition questions, album/collaboration knowledge and specialist writing/production/history questions are separated. Album and song titles are retained as proper names rather than translated into invented release titles. No lyrics are reproduced.

Editorial corrections removed hints disclosing the film behind Lose Yourself and the guest on Titanium. Let It Go film recognition moved to easy and its original English performer moved to medium. BLACKPINK's first Coachella year moved to medium; the original RCA release of Heroes moved to hard. The 1999 Ricky Martin album year replaced a weak self-titled-album clue. Shared artists or titles are checked as relations, not automatically treated as repeated facts.

Writing credits, producer credits, original recording credits and later compilation or remaster dates are kept separate. Elvis's original writers and the original Blue Hawaii film are used, not credits for a later cover. The Bad Bunny video credit comes from its production company's portfolio. David Guetta's Titanium guitarist is tied to the original 2011 licensed credit included in his official video's description, not to an inferred live performer.

Unsupported alternative credits encountered in search were excluded, including an unverified Bob Marley authorship claim, an inferred Summer video director from an Outside release announcement and production credits from later Weeknd recordings. Changing view counts, sales estimates and chart totals are not questions.

The editorial and semantic review is an assistant review, not an independent human review. Automated scans nominate lexical and shared-answer candidates; editorial inspection also checks named song/work relations and bilingual answers. Every completed protected bank is included in comparisons. No completed bank was edited to resolve a candidate.
